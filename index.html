Introducing California's Greatest Cliffs

What is so unique about West Cliff? How are the cliffs different from the others? Why is West Cliff considered the greatest cliff of all of them? It is because it is not just the highest cliff but also the most accessible cliff for visitors and hikers.

West Cliff

The west cliff is the most popular and most visited site for hiking and sightseeing. It offers trails, viewpoints, and scenic vistas that attract visitors year-round.
